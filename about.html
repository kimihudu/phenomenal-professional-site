THE CRAFT

Where Precision Meets Passion

At Phenomenal Tailoring & Alteration, we believe clothing should fit the individual — not the other way around. Every garment tells a story, and our job is to make sure it tells yours perfectly.

Lead artisan Lisa brings years of expertise to every project. Whether it's a delicate wedding gown, a tailored business suit, or a beloved vintage dress, each piece receives the same dedication to craft and precision.

We're located in the heart of Georgetown, Ontario, and proud to serve our community with same-day service when you need it most.
